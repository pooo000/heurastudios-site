The library the app's Datasets page has (app.heurastudios.com, #/datasets, behind log-in): the title and a featured row, then search and the topics, then each topic on its own island, a card for each set. A card opens its preview: three of its rows with their credits, how its rows were picked, its source and license. Downloads stay in the app. The sets are _data/datasets.json (made from the app with _tools/make-datasets.py); the featured row and the topics' icons are _data/datasets_page.yml. datasets.js runs search, the topic filter and the previews (); with no script every topic shows, and a card's link opens its preview as the page's target. {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign lib = site.data.datasets -%} {%- assign icons = site.data.datasets_page.icons -%} {%- capture arrow %}{% endcapture -%} {%- comment %} each topic's icon, once; every tile uses its topic's (datasets.css draws them: line icons, 1.3 strokes) {% endcomment %}
